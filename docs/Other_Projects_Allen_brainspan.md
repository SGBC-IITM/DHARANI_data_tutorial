## Allen developing human atlas

The Allen Brain Atlas, a flagship initiative of the Allen Institute for Brain Science, is a comprehensive and continuously expanding online public resource that integrates a vast array of data modalities relevant to brain structure and function across multiple species, including mouse, human, and non-human primates. This atlas serves as a foundational resource for the neuroscience community by providing open access to extensive gene expression data, neural connectivity information, detailed neuroanatomical atlases, and characterizations of brain cell types.

 A cornerstone of the Allen Brain Atlas is its collection of detailed anatomical reference atlases for mouse and human brains, providing standardized 3D coordinate frameworks and comprehensive annotations of brain structures. 

  the Allen Institute offers comprehensive computational tools through the AllenSDK and well-documented Application Programming Interfaces (APIs), enabling researchers to programmatically access, analyze, and visualize the vast amounts of data housed within the Allen Brain Atlas.

The BrainSpan Reference Atlases are full-color, high-resolution, Web-based digital brain atlases accompanied by a systematic, hierarchically organized taxonomy of developing human brain structures.
