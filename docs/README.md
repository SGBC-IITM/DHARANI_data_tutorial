# Introduction
**The DHARANI online platform**, accessible at https://brainportal.humanbrain.in/publicview/index.html, serves as the primary interface for visualizing and exploring the unique 3D reconstructed histological data of the developing human brain during the second trimester. This platform allows users to view the reconstructed fetal brain in the three fundamental anatomical planes: axial, coronal, and sagittal, providing a comprehensive volumetric understanding akin to that obtained from MRI. A key feature of the DHARANI platform is the integration of detailed neuroanatomical annotations on the histological sections, providing essential context for the visualized structures. Furthermore, the platform supports the overlay of different staining modalities, specifically Nissl and H&E, enabling researchers to directly compare various cellular and tissue characteristics within the same spatial framework. The user-friendly design of the DHARANI online platform makes this valuable resource readily accessible to researchers interested in studying the intricate processes of early human brain development.
